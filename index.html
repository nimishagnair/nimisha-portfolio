01 / Overview

About me

Reliable operations. Curious analysis. Clear communication.

I am a Data Operations Analyst at NielsenIQ, supporting Master Data Management (MDM) and market data quality across Latin America, particularly Argentina and Chile. My responsibilities include performing market data quality checks for Argentina, validating retailer data, investigating discrepancies, and coordinating with local contacts and stakeholders to resolve data issues and maintain accurate market information. As the team SPOC, I coordinate day-to-day follow-ups and monitor team KPIs to track performance, flag gaps, and support timely operational delivery. I also use Jira for issue tracking and documentation.

Separately, my academic and personal projects demonstrate my skills in SQL, Python, Pandas, Power BI, DAX, and Excel. My projects include interactive sales dashboards and WELoRaD, a research-based machine-learning ensemble developed using Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, and Random Forest. These project tools are distinct from the tools used in my NIQ role.
